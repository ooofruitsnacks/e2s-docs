## The five golden rules

{{< steps >}}

{{< step number="1" title="E2S helps. You decide." >}}
E2S gives information and suggestions. Every decision, like when to cross, what to eat or what to send, is yours.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="2" title="Keep your usual tools" >}}
Keep using your cane, guide dog, training and trusted people. E2S works **alongside** them, never instead of them.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="3" title="Double-check what matters" >}}
For medicine, allergies, money, streets and anything else important, confirm with a second source.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="4" title="Respect the people around you" >}}
Cameras and microphones can pick up other people. Use them kindly and legally.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="5" title="Emergencies: call 911" >}}
E2S can't call for help on its own. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
{{< /step >}}

{{< /steps >}}

## Using each feature safely

Pick a topic:

{{< tabs >}}

{{< tab name="Getting around" icon="mdi:walk" >}}
- **Directions** come from GPS and map data, which can be off by many feet, especially near tall buildings or indoors.
- **Path Alerts** can miss things: glass doors, overhanging branches, drop-offs, stairs, curbs and fast-moving cars.
- **Never cross a street based only on E2S.** Use traffic signals, your hearing, your training, and help from others.
- **Loading a saved indoor map** loads its layout. It doesn't know where you are until you start indoor guidance.
- **Transit** times and stops can change without warning. Confirm with the driver or station staff.
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab name="Food & allergies" icon="mdi:food-apple-outline" >}}
- Product info comes from **Open Food Facts**, a free database anyone can edit. It can be missing, outdated or wrong.
- Barcode results describe the product as listed, not the item in your hand. Recipes and packaging change.
- **If you have a serious allergy, always confirm the label** with a sighted person, the maker or a pharmacist, and keep your emergency medicine with you.
- Recipe times and temperatures are guides. Use safe food handling, and be careful around heat and knives.
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab name="Reading" icon="mdi:text-recognition" >}}
- E2S says **"Unsure"** when text is hard to read. Treat those lines as guesses.
- If E2S gives two possible readings, assume either one could be right until you confirm.
- **Medicine labels, doses and dates:** always confirm with a pharmacist, doctor or trusted person.
- Money, bills and legal documents: double-check before acting on them.
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab name="Camera & sound" icon="mdi:camera-outline" >}}
- Scene descriptions and object finding are **AI guesses**. They can miss things or name them wrong.
- Sound alerts, like sirens, horns or backup beeps, are helpers. They won't catch every sound.
- Low light, glare, rain and a dirty camera lens make results worse.
- Long camera or LiDAR use drains the battery and can make your phone warm. Keep it charged when you go out.
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab name="Calls & messages" icon="mdi:message-text-outline" >}}
- E2S reads back messages and asks before sending. **Listen to the whole read-back before you say yes.**
- You're responsible for what you send and who you call.
- Only let E2S read messages out loud when you're comfortable with people nearby hearing them.
{{< /tab >}}

## Respecting other people

- **Follow recording laws.** Some places require permission before you record someone. E2S saves photos only when you ask it to.
- **Don't read other people's private things** without their permission, like their mail, screens or documents.
- **Store mapping and scans** can capture people and private spaces. Scan only places you're allowed to, and follow store rules.

## Things you can't do with E2S

- Break the law, or help someone else break it
- Stalk, harass, spy on or secretly record anyone
- Use E2S to drive or operate vehicles or machinery
- Pass off a changed version as the official E2S Assistant
- Try to harm the services E2S connects to, for example by spamming or overloading them

## Your own keys and accounts

Some optional features ask for **your own** key or token, like Brave Search or Apify.

- You're responsible for following that service's rules and paying any costs it charges.
- Your keys are stored in your iPhone's secure Keychain, and **Erase all E2S data** removes them.
- Never share your keys with anyone.

## For caretakers

Caretakers are welcome to help set up E2S.

- Use **Lock Settings with Face ID** to stop settings being changed by accident.
- Go through the permission tutorial together. **Every permission is optional.**
- Respect the user's privacy and independence. E2S belongs to the person using it.
