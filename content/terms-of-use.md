## Features: What You Agree To By Using Them

{{< tab name="Navigation" icon="mdi:walk" >}}
- **Directions** come from GPS and Apple MapKit data, __E2S Assistant__ does not have control over map accuracy or route accuracy and is not responsible for misdirections.
- **Path Alerts** can miss things and is not always perfect. __E2S Assistant__ is not responsible for a missed hazard or warning.
- **Never cross a street based only on E2S.** Use traffic signals, your hearing, your training, and help from others or guide animals.
- **Loading a saved indoor map** loads its layout. It doesn't know where you are until you start indoor guidance. __E2S Assistant__ can misjudge room size and give false directions.
- **Transit** times and stops can change without warning. Confirm with the driver or station staff. __E2S Assistant__ is not responsible for missed trains or ticket refunds.

{{< tab name="Nutrition" icon="mdi:food-apple-outline" >}}
- Product info comes from **Open Food Facts**, a free database anyone can edit. Information can be missing, outdated or wrong. Please use your brain.
- Barcode results describe the product as listed from the barcode, not the item in your hand. Recipes and packaging change or can be tampered.
- **If you have a serious allergy, always confirm the label** with a sighted person, the maker or a pharmacist, and keep your emergency medicine with you.
- Recipe times and temperatures are guides. Use safe food handling, and be careful around heat and knives.
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab name="Reading" icon="mdi:text-recognition" >}}
- If __E2S Assistant__ says **"Unsure"** when text is hard to read. Treat those lines as guesses.
- If __E2S Assistant__ gives two possible readings, assume either one could be right until you confirm.
- **Medicine Labels, Doses and Dates:** always confirm with a pharmacist, doctor or trusted person.
- **Money, Bills and Legal Documents:** double-check before acting on them. Always confirm with a trusted person, __E2S Assistant__ can't provide money, investing, gambling, or legal advice. If you ask __E2S Assisant__ for information or advice on any of those topics please ensure __E2S Assistant__ is reading the proper information and treat all responses as text to speech. Returned answers aren't financial advice. 
{{< /tab >}}

{{< tab name="Camera & sound" icon="mdi:camera-outline" >}}
- Scene descriptions is provided by camera and LiDAR, __E2S Assistant__ uses the on device AI model and the YOLOv3Int8LUT ML model for object classification and object grouping. They can miss things or name them wrong.
- Sound alerts, like sirens, horns or backup beeps, are helpers. They won't catch every sound.
- Low light, glare, rain and a dirty camera lens make results worse and have the potential to return the wrong information and or warnings from __E2S Assistant__.
- Long camera or LiDAR use drains the battery and can make your phone warm. Keep it charged before heavy use and keep ventilated. __E2S Assitant__ is not responsible for any battery damage.
