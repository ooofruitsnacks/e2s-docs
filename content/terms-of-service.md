## 8. Outside services

To do certain jobs, E2S sends **only the information needed** for that job to these services:

| Service | Used for | What's sent |
|---|---|---|
| Apple (Maps, Siri, Apple Intelligence, iCloud) | Directions, places, voice, optional backup | Searches, routes, and your location while you're navigating |
| HERE and OpenStreetMap-based maps | Maps, walking and transit routes | Searches and location for the route |
| Open Food Facts | Product and allergen info | The barcode you scanned |
| Brave Search (your key, optional) | Web search answers | Your search question |
| Apify (your token, optional) | Product lookups | Your product search |

Each service has its own terms and privacy policy. I'm not responsible for how they run or what they return.
