__E2S Assistant__ is a free open-source project developed and maintained by __Owen Edwards__. All information entered into __E2S Assistant__ is never shared with the developer, Apple, or outside entities. Your information and data is stored locally on your device, with the option to store information in your I-cloud account. There are no hidden trackers and no data is collected or sold. 
  
## Privacy Concerns

__E2S Assistant__ prides itself in the data practices implemented and transparency to the users. Any and all privacy concerns with __E2S Assistant__ can be addressed in the following sections. If you have more questions or concerns please reach out via email (467487@pm.me). All data settings can be controlled within the settings tab in __E2S Assistant__ or in the settings of your phone. 

### Camera

The front and back camera's are used __ONLY__ for in app features. The front facing camera is used for Face-ID verification, this setting can be toggled off by going to __Settings > Security,Privacy,Camera > Security and Privacy > Toggle "Lock Settings with Face ID"__. The rear facing camera's are used for environmental monitoring and guidance, to read text, scan barcodes, and for live alerts/warnings of your walking path. The LiDAR sensor is used to ensure accuracy with measurement and depth readings. The camera can also be used to take pictures, those pictures are saved directly to your Iphone's gallery. There is not a duplicate copy saved anywhere else.

### Audio

Your microphone is used to detect noises such as sirens or reverse noises, chimes, honks, people talking, and for __E2S__ to actively listen to what you are saying. Your microphone is never used to record your conversations. There are no micrphone logs of your conversations. 

### GPS Location

Your GPS data is used to provide foot by foot navigational accuracy. Your exact location is only shared between __E2S Assistant__ and your device. E2S uses a blend of a custom Apple MapKit, HERE Maps, OpenStreetMaps, Organic Maps, OPNVKarte, OpenRailway Maps, CycIOSM. Routes, Waymarked Trails, OpenTOPOMaps, Apple Trails, and Apple Maps framework to provide navigational support. E2S randomizes your exact location to a 160 foot radius when sharing your location with these map kits to protect your data and location from being tracked.  

### Contacts

E2S requests for access to your contacts so it can perform tasks like texting, calling, emailing, reading messages etc. but __E2S Assistant__ never tracks any data related to your contacts, contacts interactions or conversations. 

### Tracking

No tracking. On anything. Not now. Not ever. Love Owen.

### Data Collection

Data is only collected from opted in Beta testers. No data is collected, stored, sold, transeferred, or used to train models.

## Data Protection

E2S follows all export compliance laws, the standard encryption algorithm used by __E2S Assistant__ is approved and recognized by IEEE, IETF, ISO, ITU, ETSI, 3GPP, TIA, and GSMA. These encryption practices are used to harden security defenses from outside threats accessing your data.

**Allowing App Access**: All app access can be found and configured in app. Go to __Settings > "Start Permission Tutorial" > or scroll down and press "Change Access In iPhone Settings"__.
